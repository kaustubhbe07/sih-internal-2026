Simple Workflow

How TrueMark Works

From design to verification — streamline your entire credentialing process in four simple steps.

01

Configure Schema

Define custom metadata fields (like roll number, department, and CGPA) dynamically for your institution.

02

Issue in Bulk

Upload a CSV file or enter details manually to issue cryptographically signed, chained records on the ledger.

03

Secure Chaining

Each degree is SHA-256 hashed with the previous record and embedded inside an auto-generated PDF certificate.

04

Verify Instantly

Recruiters scan the QR code to run a full chain audit, validating Ed25519 signatures and revocation status.
